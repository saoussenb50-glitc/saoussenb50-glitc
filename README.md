## Featured project

### EnquiryOps — AI Enquiry Operations Copilot

A proof-of-work system that converts unstructured service enquiries into structured, traceable operational cases.

It demonstrates:

- structured extraction
- deterministic routing
- human review gates
- separate reply approval
- duplicate protection
- prompt-injection flagging
- failure recovery
- follow-up handling
- transactional audit history
- measured evaluation on synthetic cases

**Prototype evaluation:** 88.3% field extraction accuracy, 100% routing accuracy on 60 fixed synthetic evaluation cases, and 100% duplicate precision/recall on the authored duplicate test set.

→ [Explore EnquiryOps](https://github.com/saoussenb50-glitc/enquiryops-ai-enquiry-copilot)

## What I'm exploring

I am particularly interested in overlooked operational bottlenecks inside SMBs — problems involving repetitive enquiries, documents, handoffs, scheduling, qualification, follow-up, and administrative decision-making.

The goal is not automation for its own sake.

The goal is to find a narrow operational problem, understand its failure modes, and build the smallest reliable system that removes the friction while keeping humans in control.
